FaceRoll Terms of Use and Privacy Notice

Effective October 5, 2026

1. Purpose and authorized use

FaceRoll is an educational attendance system for authorized students, instructors, and administrators. You must provide accurate account information and use the system only for legitimate course enrollment, attendance, and administration. Instructor and administrator accounts may be used only by people authorized by their institution.

2. Account responsibilities

Keep your sign-in credentials private and promptly report suspected unauthorized access to your institution or project administrator. You are responsible for activity performed through your account. Do not impersonate another person, share attendance invitations improperly, or attempt to bypass FaceRoll security controls.

3. Attendance and facial recognition

FaceRoll may use facial recognition to help identify enrolled students and record attendance. Recognition results can be incorrect and must be reviewable by the responsible instructor. FaceRoll should not be the only record used for a decision that materially affects a student. Institutions are responsible for providing required notices, obtaining any required consent, and offering an appropriate alternative attendance method.

4. Information FaceRoll processes

  • Account information, including name, email address, role, and authentication identifiers.
  • Course, enrollment, session, invitation, and attendance records.
  • Classroom location settings configured by an instructor, including coordinates and an attendance radius.
  • Facial templates generated from enrollment photos. Enrollment photos are submitted to generate templates; the templates are stored on the instructor recognition computer in encrypted form for classroom matching.
  • Limited browser cache data used to keep the dashboard responsive during brief connection interruptions.

FaceRoll uses this information to authenticate users, manage courses, support attendance check-in, perform authorized recognition, display reports, maintain security, and troubleshoot the service. Facial templates are biometric data and must not be reused for unrelated monitoring, identification, advertising, or surveillance.

5. Data access, retention, and deletion

Authorized instructors and administrators can access records for the courses they manage. Data remains available as needed to operate the course and meet the institution's recordkeeping requirements. Course deletion can remove its roster, invitations, sessions, and attendance records. Requests to correct or delete account, attendance, or facial enrollment data should be directed to the responsible instructor or institution administrator.

6. Course invitations and check-in

Course QR codes and invitation links are intended for eligible students. An invitation may expire, be revoked, or be replaced. A student who has already enrolled remains enrolled until removed from the course. Attendance check-in is available only when the student is enrolled and the instructor has an eligible session open.

7. Acceptable use

You may not disrupt the service; access data without permission; upload malicious content; falsify attendance; reverse engineer security controls; or collect, export, or disclose student or biometric data without authorization. You must follow applicable institutional policies and laws when using FaceRoll.

8. Service availability and changes

FaceRoll is under active development and may experience errors, interruptions, or changes. Instructors should verify attendance records and maintain a manual fallback when needed. These terms may be updated as the system changes; a revised effective date will identify the current version.

9. Agreement and questions

By creating an account and checking the agreement box, you confirm that you have read and accept these terms and this privacy notice. For questions or requests about FaceRoll data, contact the FaceRoll project team or your institution's administrator before using the system.
